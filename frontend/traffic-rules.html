Traffic rules worth remembering

Small decisions behind the wheel protect everyone sharing the road.

Wear a helmet

Motorcycle riders and passengers should wear a properly fastened helmet on every trip.

Put the phone away

Do not use a handheld phone while driving. Pull over before reading or replying.

Respect signals

Stop behind the line at a red light and follow lane markings and officer directions.

Control your speed

Stay within the posted limit and slow down around crossings, schools, and intersections.
