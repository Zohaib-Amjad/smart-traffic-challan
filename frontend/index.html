Road awareness

Traffic rules for everyday driving

Safe roads are built from ordinary habits. Keep these essentials in mind whether you are driving across the city or taking a longer route.

Wear a helmet

Motorcycle riders and passengers should wear a properly fastened helmet on every trip.

Do not use your phone

Put the phone away while driving. Pull over safely before reading or replying.

Follow signals

Stop behind the line at red lights and follow lane markings and officer directions.

Control your speed

Stay within the posted limit and slow down near schools, crossings, and intersections.

Keep safe distance

Leave enough space to brake smoothly when traffic slows or conditions change.

Carry valid documents

Keep your driving licence and vehicle documents available when travelling.
